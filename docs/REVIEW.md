# Review against the book (November 2026)

Each of the 166 events was checked against the text of *The Sealed Nectar* (English, Darussalam), with the English PDF's page numbers matching `source.page`. For each event the review:

- removed or corrected statements that the book does not make, using the book's own names, numbers and order of events;
- corrected Hijri dates, date precision and section headings and pages where they did not match the book;
- re-checked the Arabic and Urdu text against the corrected English (meaning, names, spellings, Urdu letters, ﷺ after mentions of the Prophet, exact Qur'an and hadith wording in Arabic);
- added `references`: the Qur'an verses, hadith collections and Seerah works the book cites for that event.

Limits:

- The Urdu EPUB used for the review was a failed OCR scan (about 1% of the text readable), so the Urdu was checked against the English edition, not against the Urdu edition.
- The first-revelation passage is missing from the English text that was used, so that event could not be checked.

Below is the full change log, grouped by batch of events, with "for human review" items at the end of each batch.
